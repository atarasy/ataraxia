# registry

**Clauses 1, 6, 13 and 55, and specification §17.**

The endpoint registry resolves and does not rank.

## Why it exists

It came out of the clause review on 2026-09-09. Reviewing clause 1 produced a
proposal from the founder that a registry of commerce information, queried by
agents as a search engine is queried by people, could be the place of
discovery, so that conformance to the protocols becomes what optimising for
search once was.

That is the intent layer, and the preamble predicts it exactly: software that
holds the seat will be asked, every quarter, to convert it into rent. The
founder chose the other thing, a registry that answers "which merchants speak
Valence, and where" and nothing else. This suite is the line between the two.

## The one test every probe applies

Does the answer depend on anything but the question? An order that means
something, a field that scores, a query by what a person wants, or an answer
that differs by who asks, and the registry has become the intent layer under
the name of a directory.

## What a failure means

**Not key order**: an order that rewards something, even being early.
**A score or a flag**: a rank with a name that sounds like a fact.
**A query by intent**: the registry has decided what a person sees.
**Different answers to different callers**: a recommendation.
**Only the marked listed**: clause 55 is false, and the mark has become a gate.

## What it does not reach

Who operates the registry. The same is open for the layer that resolves a key
to a host, and the two are related: wherever a deployment puts its default
host, a registry operated by that same party is a registry with an interest in
the answer. Whether the registry sits with a host, with the foundation, or with
nobody in particular is open, and it is the question that decides whether
"shared and neutral" is a fact or a hope. (This paragraph cited clause 2 for it; clause 2 is identity, and the registry answers to clause 1.) No probe here can reach
it, because it is a fact about an organisation and not about a response.
