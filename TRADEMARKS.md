The MIT licence covers the text and code in this repository. It does not grant
any right to use the names below.

## The names

**Ataraxia** — the constitution, the conformance mark, and the foundation that
will hold them.

**Atarasy** — the reference hub.

**Valence** — the specification and engine.

## What you may do without permission

- State that your implementation **conforms to Ataraxia**, once it passes the
  public conformance tests. This is the intended use of the mark and needs no
  licence beyond passing.
- Refer to these projects by name in prose, comparison, criticism, research and
  reporting. Nominative use is not restricted.
- Fork any repository here. Forking is a right under the licence and is
  described in the constitution itself.

## What requires permission

- Using **Atarasy** as the name of a distributed build, a service, an
  application listing, a company, a product or a domain.
- Using **Ataraxia** or **Valence** in a way that suggests your implementation
  is certified, endorsed or maintained by this project when it is not.
- Logos and wordmarks.

## Why the mark is enforced

The mark is how the constitution is enforced on parties we have no other hold
over. A build that stops honouring the constitution loses the mark; that is the
only sanction, and it is deliberately the only one.

The mark attaches to **software and hosts**. It never attaches to people. No
mark grading members will be created, and merchant endpoints and gift lineage
do not discriminate on it — see clause 64 of the constitution.

## Status

These names are **unregistered**. No trademark application has been filed, and
nothing here should be read as a claim that one has. Registration will be
considered when there is a reason to enforce, not before.

What exists today is the common-law position: the names are used by this
project, held personally by Yoichiro Hara, and licensed to conforming
implementations on the terms above. They are intended to transfer to the
foundation once it is established.

This repository also sits, provisionally, under the organisation named after
the reference hub. The constitution, its conformance tests and these marks move
to a neutral organisation when the foundation is established.
